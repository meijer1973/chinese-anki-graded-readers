# Novel Bible — 上海失物柜案

Series: 林安 journalist urban-fantasy crime.

## Core case

海安旧物公司 uses lost-property management, client contracts, passwords, and fear to control vulnerable clients. 方敏 discovers the scheme and disappears. 宋岚 tries to warn 林安 before her phone is pulled toward the 失物柜.

## Fantasy mechanism

`失物柜` returns objects tied to what a person is losing. It can guide investigation but cannot be used as public/legal proof. It is revealed as one of several `记录口` connected to a hidden civic memory/record system.

## Arc movement

白先生 speaks directly to 林安. 何青 reveals that a fifteen-year-old unpublished article already named 旧档会 and 旧城门. The series begins moving from object-level low fantasy toward pattern-level and institutional fantasy.
