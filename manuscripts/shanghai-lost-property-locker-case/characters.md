# Characters

- 林安: journalist/crime reporter. Protects sources while searching for publishable truth.
- 陈雨: police contact. Pushes legal procedure and grows more willing to trust 林安's impossible clues as leads, not proof.
- 何青: editor. Reveals prior newsroom connection to the old 旧档会 story.
- 宋岚: recurring source/witness. Moves from fear toward testimony.
- 吴梅: station guard and witness.
- 方敏: company employee/source, missing witness, later rescued.
- 夏宁: victimized shop owner/client.
- 曹卓: visible corporate antagonist.
- 白先生 / 白策: emerging arc antagonist; former journalist who now manages truth through hidden records.
- 老赵: ambiguous operator/repairman connected to older mechanisms.
- 阿洛: black-market technical contact.
- 江小北: photographer/source of ordinary visual evidence.
