# Creative Preflight — 上海失物柜案

## Premise alternatives considered

1. Lost-property cabinet returns evidence from crimes not yet reported.
2. Newspaper archive removes names from old articles.
3. Fake future headline site predicts crimes.
4. Mirror street makes rumors visible.

## Chosen story shape

A procedural missing-witness / corporate blackmail case around a Shanghai lost-property cabinet. The fantasy mechanism stays small: the `失物柜` returns objects tied to what people are losing—names, phones, files, safety, source protection.

## Variation budget from prior story

- Investigation structure: lost-property office, witness intimidation, package evidence, warehouse search.
- Emotional wound: not only fear of publishing, but fear that records can rewrite a person into a criminal.
- Antagonist pressure: visible company manager plus the first direct voice contact from 白先生.
- Fantasy mechanism: object-return / record-return rather than water list.
- Arc seed: six prior mechanisms are explicitly connected as recording mouths/doors of the same hidden city system.

## Reader question

Can 林安 find 宋岚 and 方敏, expose the company, and avoid letting 白先生 control the rhythm of the truth?
