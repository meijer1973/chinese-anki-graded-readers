# Outline

## Chapter 1: 雨票

Story reason: Discovery. 林安 finds the strange ticket while delivering a package and learns that 陈雨 may be behind the impossible door.

Change: Ordinary rainy work becomes a personal mystery.

Hook: The door speaks with 陈雨's voice.

## Chapter 2: 门后

Story reason: Cost. 林安 learns the rule from 周叔 and opens the door anyway.

Change: She stops treating the ticket as a strange object and accepts that a memory may be lost.

Hook: 陈雨 is alive, but returning her will make someone forget.

## Chapter 3: 记住

Story reason: Choice and release. 林安 chooses what memory to give and brings 陈雨 back.

Change: The frightening place becomes a place of promise.

Ending: The rain stops; the ticket becomes ordinary, but the characters keep the memory by telling the story.
