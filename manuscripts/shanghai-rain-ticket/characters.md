# Characters

## 林安

Role: 快递员.
Want: Find 陈雨 and prove that one person can still matter in a busy city.
Fear: Being too late, or forgetting someone important.

## 陈雨

Role: former 店员 and 林安's friend.
Want: Come back without making everyone forget her.
Secret: She used the 雨票 once before.

## 阿明

Role: 咖啡师.
Want: Keep the 咖啡店 safe.
Function: Gives warmth, local knowledge, and a reason for 林安 not to go alone.

## 周叔

Role: 清洁工 and 守门人.
Want: Keep the 影门 hidden.
Function: Explains the rule and creates pressure without becoming a flat villain.
