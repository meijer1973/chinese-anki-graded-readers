# 雨票 Novel Bible

## Premise

林安 is a young delivery worker in Shanghai. On a rainy night near the Huangpu River, she finds a strange ticket that opens an `影门`. The ticket can bring back one missing person, but each use takes away one important memory from someone nearby.

## Target Reader Level

Core known words plus approved stretch packs. Occasional unknowns are allowed only within the five-token chapter budget.

## Shape

No fixed chapter count or word count. This trial uses three short chapters because the story has three natural turns: discovery, cost, choice.

## Point Of View

Close third person around 林安.

## Characters

- 林安: 快递员. Wants to finish work and stop feeling like she is only running through other people's lives.
- 陈雨: missing friend. Her promise to 林安 is the emotional center.
- 阿明: 咖啡师 at 旧梦 咖啡店. Practical, afraid, but loyal.
- 周叔: 清洁工 and hidden 守门人. Knows the rule of the 雨票.

## Setting Map

- 上海: city frame.
- 黄浦江 / 江边: first discovery and final choice.
- 地铁站 / 站台: public pressure and city movement.
- 小巷: the 影门 appears here.
- 旧梦 咖啡店: human warmth and recurring base.
- 屋顶: final decision space.

## Fantasy Rule

When rain falls after deep night, the 雨票 can show an `影门`. Opening it can return a missing person, but someone must lose or give up one important `记忆`.

## Central Conflict

林安 can save 陈雨 only if she accepts the loss of a memory and refuses 周叔's safer silence.

## Emotional Arc

林安 begins as tired and careful, becomes afraid but curious, then chooses active courage over safe forgetting.

## Recurring Phrases

- 不要 只 看 路
- 这 张 票 不是 车票
- 门后 有 一个 人
- 我 会 记住 你

## Risky Concepts To Avoid

Avoid elaborate magic systems, monsters, politics, and technical transit details. Keep the fantasy small: one ticket, one door, one rule.

## Quality Risks

- Too much explaining instead of scene movement.
- Using Shanghai place names only as decoration.
- Making 周叔 a pure villain instead of a tired protector.
- Forcing zero unknowns when one useful word would carry the scene better.
