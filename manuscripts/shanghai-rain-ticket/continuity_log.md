# Continuity Log

## Initial Anchors

- The strange object is the 雨票.
- The fantasy place is the 影门 in the 小巷 near 旧梦 咖啡店.
- The cost is one important 记忆.
- 陈雨 is missing before chapter 1 and connected to the ticket.
- 周叔 knows the rule and watches the door.

## Open Questions

- What did 陈雨 give up when she used the ticket before?
- What will 林安 choose to lose or keep?
- Can 阿明 help without opening the door himself?

## Chapter Updates

### Chapter 1

- 林安 is a 快递员 delivering a 包裹 to 旧梦 咖啡店.
- The package names 陈雨, who disappeared three months earlier.
- 林安 finds the 雨票 under a 路灯 near the 咖啡店.
- 阿明 recognizes the ticket and confirms 陈雨 has been gone for three months.
- The 影门 appears in the 小巷, showing 陈雨's 倒影 and voice.
- Open question: why does the ticket answer to 林安?

### Chapter 2

- 阿明 brings 林安 to 周叔, a 清洁工 who is secretly the 守门人.
- 周叔 explains the rule: the 影门 can return a lost person, but it takes one important 记忆.
- 周叔 claims he hides the door to protect people.
- 陈雨 appears behind 黑雾 and 白光.
- 林安 chooses to enter despite the cost.
- Open question: what memory will the door take?

### Chapter 3

- 陈雨 used the 雨票 to find her father, and the city began to forget her.
- The door chooses 林安 because she still remembers 陈雨's voice.
- 林安 gives the door her fear from the missed phone call, not 陈雨's name.
- 陈雨 returns. 林安 loses some darkness/fear but remembers her friend.
- The 雨票 becomes an ordinary 车票 and is kept in 林安's 笔记本.
- Ending state: 旧梦 咖啡店 reopens; the 江边 location changes from threat to remembered promise.
