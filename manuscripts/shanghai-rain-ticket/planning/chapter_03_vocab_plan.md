# Chapter 03 Vocabulary Plan

## Chapter Purpose

Resolve the choice and make the location emotionally changed.

## Scene Goal

Bring 陈雨 back without losing the promise at the center of the friendship.

## Conflict

The door wants a memory; 周叔 wants safety; 林安 wants truth and return.

## Emotional Turn

Fear becomes courage and grief becomes warmth.

## Main Locations

屋顶, 江边, 黄浦江, 咖啡店.

## Characters And Roles

林安 / 快递员. 陈雨 / friend. 阿明 / 咖啡师. 周叔 / 守门人.

## Core Known Words To Reuse

选择, 记住, 忘记, 承认, 相信, 回来, 爱, 朋友, 声音, 光, 黑, 手, 心, 笑, 哭, 停, 生活, 开始.

## Stretch Words To Introduce

- 勇气 / general fiction / choice
- 承诺 / general fiction / ending promise
- 光亮 / general fiction / visual turn
- 温暖 / general fiction / ending emotional note
- 屋顶 / setting / final place
- 倒影 / book-specific / fantasy image

## Stretch Words To Repeat From Earlier Chapters

雨票, 影门, 记忆, 黄浦江, 咖啡店, 阿明, 陈雨, 周叔.

## Risky Unavailable Concepts To Avoid

Avoid sacrifice melodrama; keep the choice small and clear.

## Chapter Hook

No hook needed; this is the ending.

## End-Of-Chapter Change

The strange city place becomes a remembered promise.
