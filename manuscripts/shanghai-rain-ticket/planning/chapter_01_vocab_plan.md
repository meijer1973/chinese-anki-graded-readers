# Chapter 01 Vocabulary Plan

## Chapter Purpose

Show ordinary Shanghai work becoming an impossible personal mystery.

## Scene Goal

林安 wants to deliver a 包裹 to 旧梦 咖啡店 and go home.

## Conflict

The 雨票 names 陈雨, who should not be there, and the city seems to answer.

## Emotional Turn

Tired duty becomes fear and hope.

## Main Locations

黄浦江, 江边, 地铁站, 小巷, 旧梦 咖啡店.

## Characters And Roles

林安 / 快递员. 阿明 / 咖啡师. 陈雨 / missing friend.

## Core Known Words To Reuse

我, 她, 他, 在, 看, 看到, 说, 问, 走, 跑, 找, 电话, 照片, 朋友, 记得, 忘记, 事情, 晚上, 声音, 手, 心, 门, 路, 光, 黑, 回来, 需要.

## Stretch Words To Introduce

- 上海 / setting / city frame
- 黄浦江 / setting / discovery place
- 地铁站 / setting / movement
- 小巷 / setting / hidden door
- 咖啡店 / setting / base
- 快递员 / profession / 林安's role
- 咖啡师 / profession / 阿明's role
- 包裹 / urban object / delivery task
- 车票 / urban object / ordinary cover
- 雨票 / book-specific / strange object
- 影门 / low fantasy / mystery hook
- 记忆 / general fiction / fantasy cost later

## Stretch Words To Repeat From Earlier Chapters

None.

## Risky Unavailable Concepts To Avoid

Avoid transit bureaucracy, complex weather descriptions, and technical magic terms.

## Chapter Hook

The door calls with 陈雨's voice.

## End-Of-Chapter Change

林安 can no longer call this a normal delivery.
