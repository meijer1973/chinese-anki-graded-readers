# 小店怎么活下来 — nonfiction graded reader bible

## Identity

- English working title: How a Small Shop Survives
- Slug: `small-shop-survival-economics`
- Mode: standalone case-based nonfiction / explanatory business-economics reader
- Continuity: not a 林安 Rain Gate continuity story; this uses the same manuscript/export procedure only.
- Topic: how a small Shanghai shop survives through price, cost, profit, cash flow, customers, trust, inventory, competition, loans, tax, policy, management, sales, consumption, and production.

## Reader promise

The reader learns practical economics through one concrete repeated setting: a small shop on a Shanghai street. Abstract concepts are introduced only when they are tied to visible shop actions.

## Vocabulary policy

- Core known words: 1,100 active known words.
- Approved stretch layers: general fiction, Shanghai/urban setting, professions/social roles, urban objects, business/economics.
- Forbidden unknown target: 0.
- Actual forbidden unknowns after local validation: 0.

## Style rule

Use short explanatory scenes, repeated concrete vocabulary, and clear cause-effect language. Avoid textbook density, macroeconomic abstraction, and theory terms not supported by the approved vocabulary.
