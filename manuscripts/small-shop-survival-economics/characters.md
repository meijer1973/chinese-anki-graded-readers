# Roles / explanatory actors

This nonfiction reader uses recurring roles rather than a named fiction cast.

- 店主: owner/decision-maker; carries price, cost, inventory, loan, and management choices.
- 员工 / 店员: service and wage perspective; shows that labor is both cost and capability.
- 顾客 / 客户: demand, trust, repeated transactions, and service expectations.
- 供应商: cost, quality, timing, and inventory risk.
- 房东: location and rent pressure.
- 银行: loan, interest, repayment, and external trust in the accounts.
- 政府 / 法律: tax, policy, and rules around business activity.
