# Creative Preflight — 小店怎么活下来

## Vocabulary Profile

Public mode: core known words plus approved general fiction, Shanghai setting, profession/social-role, urban object, and business/economics stretch packs.

## Alternatives Considered

1. A story about one shop owner trying to save a failing shop.
2. A journalist-style report about several small businesses in one street.
3. A practical nonfiction guide built around one repeated Shanghai shop.
4. A dialogue-only lesson between a boss and a worker.

## Rejected Ideas

- The failing-shop drama would need more personal conflict and legal/business terms than the target vocabulary can comfortably support.
- The multi-business report would introduce too many locations and people for a first business/economics reader.
- The dialogue-only lesson would risk sounding like a vocabulary exercise.

## Chosen Shape

A standalone case-based nonfiction reader: one normal Shanghai small shop becomes the repeated example for price, cost, profit, cash flow, customers, inventory, competition, loans, taxes, policy, and management.

## Reader Question

How does a small shop stay alive when money, customers, rent, wages, risk, and competition all press on it at the same time?

## Main Pressure

The shop must make many small decisions with limited money, limited space, limited information, and changing customer behavior.

## Planned Turns

- Start with the visible shop and the basic survival question.
- Move from money-in/money-out to price, cost, profit, and customer trust.
- Add pressure from rent, wages, inventory, competition, and loans.
- Show how data, policy, advertising, sales, production, and consumption connect.
- End with a synthesis: survival is not one lucky choice, but many clear decisions repeated over time.

## Variation Budget

This is not a 林安 urban-fantasy crime story. It varies the repository's reader pipeline by using nonfiction explanation, business/economics vocabulary, public-mode validation, and repeated practical examples instead of mystery, fantasy, or series continuity.
