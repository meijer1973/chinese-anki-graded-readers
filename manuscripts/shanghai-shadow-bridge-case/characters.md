# Characters — 上海影子桥案

- 林安: journalist and protagonist. She investigates while balancing publication and source protection.
- 陈雨: police contact. He insists on usable evidence: files, recordings, fingerprints, blood traces, and witness testimony.
- 何青: editor. She controls publication timing and keeps the article legally and ethically usable.
- 许白: photographer/source from earlier continuity. He gives 丁蓝's packet and later photographs the bridge confrontation.
- 丁蓝: photographer/source and victim. Her files and recordings open the case.
- 小北: external delivery worker and protected witness. His safety drives 林安's publication choices.
- 蔡明: bridge guard / informant. He first hides the truth, then gives testimony and a recording.
- 孟河: old-street manager and suspect. He uses the bridge mechanism to separate himself from direct action.
- 老马: bookstore owner / gatekeeper. He explains the shadow-bridge rule.
