# Novel Bible — 上海影子桥案

## Series position
Third 林安 journalist urban-fantasy crime story. It follows `上海雨票案` and `上海灵灯案`.

## Core premise
A photographer/source named 丁蓝 dies near a bridge after investigating old-street property files. The only visible suspect in the police video has no shadow. 林安 investigates the case as a journalist while 陈雨 builds legal evidence and 何青 controls publication risk.

## Low-fantasy mechanism
影子桥: a hidden bridge mechanism connected to black tickets, memory, and shadow. If a person gives a memory to the bridge, a shadow can act once for that person. The fantasy clue points to real evidence but is not itself treated as legal evidence.

## Ethical frame
林安 must publish enough truth to pressure the suspect, but not expose 小北 as a vulnerable witness. The final article protects the source and omits the fantasy mechanism.
