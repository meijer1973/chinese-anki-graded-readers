# Chapter 18 Vocabulary Plan — 桥上影子

## Chapter purpose
影子袭击林安，白光照出黑雨伞上的血迹。孟河被控制，小北出声求救。

## Case function
Advance the 丁蓝 / 小北 / 孟河 evidence chain.

## Journalist function
林安 interviews, confirms, protects a source, or prepares publication discipline.

## Fantasy function
Keep the fantasy small: black ticket, shadow bridge, memory, mirror/door, or no-shadow clue.

## Learning function
Repeat journalism/crime words and recurring series fantasy words naturally. Avoid adding one-off fantasy lore.
