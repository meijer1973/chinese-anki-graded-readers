## Alternatives Considered

1. Pure sect-school story: readable but too flat for a long series; risks repeating training and tests.
2. Demon-war epic: possible under current rules, but too many factions and battle terms for a first 5,000-token volume.
3. Court-political xianxia: strong pressure, but too political too early for the opening volume.
4. Fallen sword sect plus hidden founding crime: strongest shape; gives training, mystery, pressure, and a long arc.

## Chosen Story Shape

Volume 1 starts with a fallen sword sect and one new disciple who seems weak. 青禾 enters 青岚剑宗, fails the ordinary status test, is pushed toward low work, and finds a broken sword in 剑阁. The sword answers her family name and ties her personal shame to the sect's founding mystery.

## Reader Question

Why does a useless-looking disciple hear the broken sword, and what did her family owe the 山门?

## Main Pressure

青禾 wants to become a real disciple, but the sect's public hierarchy says her weak 灵根 makes her nearly worthless.

## Planned Reversals

- The weak disciple hears something stronger disciples cannot hear.
- Cleaning the 剑阁 becomes the first real test.
- The broken sword is not a prize of strength; it is a demand for truth.

## Variation Budget

- Opens with sect entry and shame, not a city case or ordinary school scene.
- Uses sword-sect hierarchy and cultivation vocabulary from `fantasy_225`.
- Keeps the first volume narrow: 青岚山门, 剑阁, 试剑台, and 后山 pressure only.
