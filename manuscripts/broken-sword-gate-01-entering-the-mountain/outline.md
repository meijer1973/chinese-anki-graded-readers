# Outline - 断剑入山

## Chapter 1 - 山门 Test

青禾 arrives at 青岚山门 with other new disciples. Her 灵根 is tested and judged weak. She is placed below the other disciples and told that hard work may still earn her a place.

End change: 青禾 enters the sect, but not with the status she imagined.

## Chapter 2 - 试剑台 Shame

青禾 trains with basic 剑招. Other disciples move more cleanly. 江白 corrects her, but his help also makes her failure public. 莫长老 treats her as a burden.

End change: 青禾 is sent to clean 剑阁 instead of joining normal evening practice.

## Chapter 3 - 剑阁 Sound

In 剑阁, old swords feel silent except one broken blade. 青禾 hears a thread of 剑气 or memory. Touching the sword shows black wind, a sealed stone door, and a woman protecting someone.

End change: 断剑 has chosen to answer her.

## Chapter 4 - Hidden 禁制

青禾 tries to hide what happened, but the sword pulls her toward a false mark or old 禁制. A small cut exposes that something in the sect's official story is wrong.

End hook: the sword speaks her family name.
