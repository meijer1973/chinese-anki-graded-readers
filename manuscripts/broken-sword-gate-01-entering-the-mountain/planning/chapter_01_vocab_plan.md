# Chapter 01 Vocabulary Plan - 山门 Test

## Chapter Purpose

Introduce 青禾, 青岚山门, the weak 灵根 judgment, and the social pressure of becoming a low-status new disciple.

## Scene Goal

青禾 wants to pass the entry test and be accepted as a real disciple.

## Conflict

The test shows weak 灵根, and sect hierarchy immediately turns that result into shame.

## Emotional Turn

青禾 arrives with hope and leaves the test determined but humiliated.

## Main Locations

- 青岚山门
- 试剑台, if the chapter needs a visible public platform

## Characters And Roles

- 沈青禾: viewpoint protagonist.
- 江白: senior disciple who applies or explains rules.
- 莫长老: authority pressure.
- 云澜: distant 掌门 presence, optional for Chapter 1.

## Core Known Words To Reuse

来, 进, 看, 说, 问, 站, 手, 心, 山, 门, 名字, 人, 孩子, 老人, 声音, 风, 晚上, 以后.

## Stretch Words To Introduce

山门, 灵根, 弟子, 长老, 师兄.

## Stretch Words To Repeat

山门, 灵根, 弟子.

## Risky Unavailable Concepts To Avoid

Detailed rank bureaucracy, many named disciples, formal ritual terminology, and dense cultivation exposition.

## Chapter Hook

The test result is weak, but 青禾 hears or feels something near the old swords that other disciples do not notice.

## End-Of-Chapter Change

青禾 is inside the sect, but her public place is lower than she expected.

## Fantasy Function

灵根 test establishes cultivation pressure.

## Learning Function

Repeat a small group of sect terms in action: 山门, 灵根, 弟子.
