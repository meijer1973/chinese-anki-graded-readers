# Characters - 断剑入山

## 沈青禾

- Role: protagonist and new disciple.
- Want: become a real sword disciple.
- Fear: she was saved by mistake and has no worth.
- First-volume turn: from seeking approval to realizing the sect has a hidden debt to her family.

## 江白

- Role: senior disciple / 师兄.
- Want: make the sect look orderly and honorable.
- First-volume function: teaches rules, applies pressure, and may become the first person to see 青禾's unusual sensitivity.

## 云澜

- Role: 掌门.
- Want: protect the sect and control dangerous truth.
- First-volume function: notices that the sword's response is serious.

## 莫长老

- Role: elder and internal pressure.
- Want: keep weak disciples quiet and preserve hierarchy.
- First-volume function: turns 青禾's weak 灵根 into public shame.

## Other New Disciples

Use sparingly. They may create social pressure at the 山门 and 试剑台, but should not become a crowd of named characters in Volume 1.
