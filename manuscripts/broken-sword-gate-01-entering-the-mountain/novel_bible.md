# Novel Bible - 断剑入山

## Premise

沈青禾, a poor village girl with a weak 灵根, enters 青岚剑宗. The sect expects her to become a low-status disciple, but an old 断剑 in 剑阁 answers her and speaks her family name.

## Point Of View

Close third person centered on 沈青禾.

## Main Characters

- 沈青禾: new disciple, ashamed of weak 灵根, stubborn and observant.
- 江白: senior disciple / 师兄 figure, disciplined and loyal to rules.
- 云澜: 掌门, controlled mentor figure who notices more than she says.
- 莫长老: conservative elder who wants weak disciples to stay quiet.

## Main Locations

- 青岚山门: arrival, testing, public judgment.
- 试剑台: visible failure and pressure.
- 剑阁: cleaning task, old swords, discovery of 断剑.
- 后山 / 后山禁地: hinted danger, not fully entered yet.

## Fantasy Mechanisms

- 灵根 marks expected cultivation ability.
- 剑气 can be heard or felt by 青禾 in an unusual way.
- 断剑 can respond to false seals and family history.
- 禁制 appears as a hidden rule or seal that stronger disciples overlook.

## Proposed Chapter Breaks

1. Arrival at 青岚山门: 青禾 is tested and judged weak.
2. First training and public shame: ordinary 剑招 fail her.
3. Night task in 剑阁: she hears the broken sword.
4. First cut of hidden 禁制: the sword speaks her family name.

Chapter count is provisional. Do not pad or compress against story logic.

## Stretch Words To Introduce Carefully

山门, 灵根, 弟子, 师兄, 剑阁, 断剑, 剑气, 剑招, 禁制, 试炼, 试剑台.

## Risky Concepts

- Too many ranks too early.
- Dense invented politics before the reader cares about 青禾.
- Explaining the whole founding crime in Volume 1.
- Treating 断剑 as a simple power-up.

## End Hook

The broken sword whispers that the 沈 family still owes the 山门 one sword.
