# Literary critic report

Decision: PASS with nonfiction constraints.

The manuscript is not literary fiction; its quality standard is clarity, pacing, and explanatory usefulness. The chapters build a coherent path from one news article to the deeper policy background: supply security, coal, oil imports, LNG, dual-carbon policy, renewables, grid/storage, EVs, Hormuz, and downstream shortages.

Strengths:

- Each chapter has one clear explanatory function.
- The abstract policy language is repeatedly grounded in factories, families, transport, agriculture, and supply chains.
- The book avoids becoming a translation of the referenced article.

Risks:

- Controlled vocabulary forces simplification of complex energy-policy terms. This is acceptable for a long read at this level.
