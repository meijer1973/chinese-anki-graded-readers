# Nonfiction bible — 《中国能源为什么要变》

## Type

Standalone nonfiction / economic-news graded reader.

## Central thesis

China’s energy policy over the past years is best understood as a three-line strategy: energy security, low-carbon transition, and industrial advantage. The 2026 Strait of Hormuz shock makes these lines visible because it turns seaborne oil and LNG dependence into concrete shortages, not only higher prices.

## Scope

- China’s energy demand and coal base.
- Oil and LNG import vulnerability.
- 2020 dual-carbon targets and 14th Five-Year Plan direction.
- Renewable power, grid, storage, EVs, and industrial policy.
- Hormuz crisis background and downstream shortages: fertilizer, helium, naphtha, LPG, jet fuel.
- Current 2026 policy balance.

## Exclusions

No dense macroeconomic model, no detailed emissions accounting, no investment advice, no translation of the referenced article.
