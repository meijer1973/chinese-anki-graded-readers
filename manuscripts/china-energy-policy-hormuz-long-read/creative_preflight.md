# Creative preflight — 《中国能源为什么要变》

## Alternatives considered

1. A narrow reader only about the Strait of Hormuz. Rejected because it would become too event-specific and vocabulary-dense.
2. A textbook chapter about China energy policy. Rejected because it would be too abstract for extensive reading.
3. A source-background long read connecting China’s energy-policy history to the current Hormuz shock. Chosen.

## Chosen shape

Standalone nonfiction graded reader. It explains the long policy arc: supply security, coal as system base, 2020 dual-carbon goals, renewables, grid/storage, EV oil-demand change, and the 2026 Hormuz supply shock.

## Reader question

Why does the current Hormuz shortage story make China’s recent energy policy easier to understand?

## Main pressure

Every chapter should turn an abstract energy-policy term into a concrete chain: source → transport → price or shortage → company/factory/family/policy response.

## Variation budget

Use repeated core terms for comprehension, but vary chapter function: policy history, supply-chain explanation, country comparison, market mechanism, current situation, and reading strategy.
