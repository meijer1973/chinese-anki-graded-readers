# Creative Preflight

## 3-5 Premise Or Scene Alternatives
1. A lost witness returns through another object case. Rejected: too close to `上海失物柜案`.
2. A fake-news future headline case. Rejected for now because it risks making journalism itself too magical too quickly.
3. A silent archive case where names vanish from records and recordings. Chosen because it extends the longer arc from objects-as-records toward institutions-as-records.
4. A police-document rewriting case. Deferred because it would move too deeply into police-system compromise before the archive layer is clear.

## Rejected Ideas And Why
The story rejects a monster, a large battle, or a hidden kingdom. The fantasy remains one small repeated mechanism: names in certain archives can become silent or blank.

## Chosen Story Shape
A retired editor brings 林安 an old article that can no longer say a dead child’s name. The case leads through newsroom guilt, old street redevelopment, a data-cleaning company, witness pressure, ordinary financial evidence, and the emerging role of 白先生 / 白策 and 旧档会.

## Why This Is Stronger Than The Alternatives
It keeps the procedural setup while developing the longer arc. The fantasy mechanism is thematically tied to journalism: truth, names, sources, publication, and the cost of silence.

## Core Reader Question
Can 林安 prove a crime when the archive itself removes the names of victims and witnesses?

## Main Character Want
林安 wants to restore the erased names without exposing vulnerable witnesses to renewed danger.

## Main Pressure
White-collar evidence can be published, but victim/source names can become weapons. 白先生 offers a controlled version of truth that would expose the company while preserving his hidden system.

## Planned Reversals
- The old editor is not only a victim; she once helped bury the story.
- 胡越 appears as a first witness, then becomes an unwilling attacker.
- 清页 looks like an ordinary reputation-management company, but it uses the hidden archive mechanism.
- The archive is not merely magical; it is a record mouth connected to previous cases.

## Emotional Movement
林安 moves from anger at silence to a more precise distinction: silence can protect people, but controlled silence can also protect criminals.

## Vocabulary Feasibility Notes
The manuscript uses the current 1500 core known words, Marcel personalized mode, journalism/crime vocabulary, Shanghai/urban terms, business/economics terms for company-account evidence, and book-specific recurring arc terms.

## Public-Quality Risks
Risk: repeated explanation of names/records. Mitigation: each chapter changes the investigation state, and the mechanism is linked to concrete objects: old reports, email, police records, account trails, a back room, and an article.

## Variation Budget
- Investigation structure: archive/data-cleaning case rather than lost-property object chain.
- Emotional wound: newsroom guilt and source-protection mistakes.
- Antagonist pressure: 白先生 directly offers 林安 a managed-truth bargain.
- Fantasy mechanism: names become silent/blank in archives and recordings.
- Moral dilemma: publishing names may restore victims but also expose witnesses.
