# Novel Bible — 上海无声档案案

## Series Position
Book 7 after `上海失物柜案`. 林安 is an established journalist/crime reporter. 陈雨 remains the police contact. 何青 carries newsroom-history pressure. 白先生 / 白策 is the emerging arc antagonist.

## Core Case
A retired editor, 唐岚, brings evidence that an old article and police record can no longer speak a child’s name. The visible criminal surface is 清页公司, a data-cleaning/reputation-management company that turns old evidence into paid silence.

## Fantasy Mechanism
`无声 档案`: names in certain old archives become blank or unspeakable when they are captured by a hidden record system. It guides the investigation but cannot be used as legal proof.

## Procedural Proof
Emails, payment records, bank-card use, contracts, invoices, police search, account links, witness testimony, recoverable old drafts, a shop ledger, old financial records, technical backup direction, and cross-case contract links.

## Arc Movement
The previous mechanisms are recast as forms of `记录口`. 许白 provides a mechanism list that points toward `旧城门`; 白先生 argues that truth must be managed; 老赵 confirms the system is older than 白策. 林安 rejects managed truth and finds a publication method that exposes the crime without casually naming vulnerable sources.
