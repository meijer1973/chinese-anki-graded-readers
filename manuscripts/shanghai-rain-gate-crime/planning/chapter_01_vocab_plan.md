# Chapter 01 Vocabulary Plan

## Chapter purpose
Start 林安 as a journalist and introduce the rain-ticket case.

## Scene goal
林安 receives the first impossible clue and chooses to investigate.

## Conflict
The clue looks small, but the city rain and old case make it dangerous.

## Emotional turn
Curiosity becomes responsibility.

## Case function
New clue.

## Fantasy function

Shanghai street, office, rain.

## Characters and roles
林安: journalist protagonist.

None.

## End-of-chapter change
