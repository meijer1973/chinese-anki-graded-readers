# Chapter 05 Vocabulary Plan

## Chapter purpose
Connect the impossible door to a human suspect.

## Scene goal
林安 and 陈雨 identify a person with a motive.

## Conflict
The suspect denies everything and someone is watching them.

## Emotional turn
The mystery becomes personal danger.

## Case function
Suspect pressure.

## Journalist function
Question, compare accounts, and avoid publishing too early.

## Fantasy function
Black mist hides part of the truth.

## Learning function
Repeat: 嫌犯, 否认, 动机, 黑雾, 证词.

## Main locations
Cafe, street, police station.

## Characters and roles
林安: journalist. 陈雨: police. Suspect: pressure point.

## Core known words to reuse
男人, 女人, 说话, 不, 知道, 怕, 真相.

## Stretch words to introduce
- 嫌犯: suspect role.
- 动机: crime reasoning word.
- 否认: interview conflict verb.

## Stretch words to repeat from earlier chapters
证人, 采访, 确认, 痕迹.

## Risky unavailable concepts to avoid
Dense motive psychology.

## Chapter hook
The suspect knows the rain ticket before 林安 shows it.

## End-of-chapter change
The suspect is no longer just a name.
