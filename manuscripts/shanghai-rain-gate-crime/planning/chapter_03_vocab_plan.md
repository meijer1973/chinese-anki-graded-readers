Bring 陈雨 into the case as police contact.

## Scene goal
林安 asks 陈雨 to check whether the old report is real.

## Conflict
陈雨 wants safety and proof; 林安 wants speed and truth.

## Emotional turn

林安: journalist. 陈雨: police contact.

## End-of-chapter change
