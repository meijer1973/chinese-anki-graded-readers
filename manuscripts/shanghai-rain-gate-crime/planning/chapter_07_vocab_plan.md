# Chapter 07 Vocabulary Plan

## Chapter purpose
Make the newsroom matter.

## Scene goal
林安 argues with the editor about what can be reported.

## Conflict
The editor wants proof; 林安 wants to warn people.

## Emotional turn
林安 sees that a report can hurt or help.

## Case function
Public reporting consequence.

## Journalist function
Draft, revise, and decide whether to publish.

## Fantasy function
The rain ticket changes words on the page.

## Learning function
Repeat: 报道, 文章, 编辑, 发布, 标题.

## Main locations
News office, desk, website.

## Characters and roles
林安: journalist. Editor: gatekeeper.

## Core known words to reuse
写, 工作, 公司, 事情, 决定, 不能.

## Stretch words to introduce
- 报道: news coverage.
- 文章: journalist output.
- 编辑: newsroom role.
- 发布: publication action.
- 标题: headline clue.

## Stretch words to repeat from earlier chapters
文件, 来源, 保密, 确认.

## Risky unavailable concepts to avoid
Industry jargon and media theory.

## Chapter hook
The headline appears before 林安 writes it.

## End-of-chapter change
The newsroom becomes part of the fantasy mechanism.
