# Chapter 10 Vocabulary Plan

## Chapter purpose
Turn investigation into chase and danger.

## Scene goal
林安 follows a trace through Shanghai at night.

## Conflict
She is also being followed.

## Emotional turn
Determination becomes fear, then courage.

## Case function
Suspect movement and physical clue.

## Journalist function
Observe, photograph, and avoid being caught.

## Fantasy function
The shadow gate changes the route.

## Learning function
Repeat: 跟踪, 追踪, 拍照, 监视, 指纹.

## Main locations
Subway station, narrow lane, riverside bridge.

## Characters and roles
林安: journalist. Follower: threat. 陈雨: backup.

## Core known words to reuse
跑, 走, 车站, 路, 后面, 小心, 快.

## Stretch words to introduce
- 跟踪: follow/tail.
- 追踪: trace a clue.
- 拍照: gather evidence.
- 监视: watching pressure.
- 指纹: physical clue.

## Stretch words to repeat from earlier chapters
痕迹, 摄像机, 录像, 黑雾.

## Risky unavailable concepts to avoid
Detailed chase mechanics.

## Chapter hook
The trace leads back to the first rain ticket.

## End-of-chapter change
林安 stops reacting and sets a trap.
