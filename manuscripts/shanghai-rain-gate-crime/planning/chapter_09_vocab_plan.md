# Chapter 09 Vocabulary Plan

## Chapter purpose
Bring in recorded evidence.

## Scene goal
林安 and 陈雨 compare testimony with audio and video.

## Conflict
The evidence contradicts what the witness said.

## Emotional turn
林安 doubts her own earlier judgment.

## Case function
Contradictory evidence.

## Journalist function
Verify before publishing a correction or follow-up.

## Fantasy function
The recording catches a voice from behind the shadow door.

## Learning function
Repeat: 录音, 录像, 证词, 判断, 镜头.

## Main locations
Police station, small office, old building.

## Characters and roles
林安: journalist. 陈雨: police. Witness: uncertain source.

## Core known words to reuse
听, 声音, 看到, 不 一样, 记录, 错.

## Stretch words to introduce
- 录音: audio evidence.
- 录像: video evidence.
- 证词: witness statement.
- 判断: assess evidence.

## Stretch words to repeat from earlier chapters
证人, 确认, 嫌犯, 动机.

## Risky unavailable concepts to avoid
Forensic technical detail.

## Chapter hook
The video shows 林安 entering before she arrived.

## End-of-chapter change
The evidence is useful but no longer simple.
