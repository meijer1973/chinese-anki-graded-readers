# Chapter 02 Vocabulary Plan

## Chapter purpose
Move the investigation from clue to person.

## Scene goal
林安 searches for someone connected to the ticket.

## Conflict
People are afraid to speak.

## Emotional turn
林安 realizes the case has witnesses, not only objects.

## Case function
Witness pressure.

## Journalist function
Interview and verify a first account.

## Fantasy function
Rain changes what people remember.

## Learning function
Repeat: 雨票, 线索, 证人, 采访, 来源.

## Main locations
Street, shop, rain-covered doorway.

## Characters and roles
林安: journalist. Witness: reluctant source.

## Core known words to reuse
问, 说, 听, 看见, 害怕, 朋友, 店.

## Stretch words to introduce
- 证人: crime role, repeat in later chapters.
- 采访: journalist action, repeat in later chapters.

## Stretch words to repeat from earlier chapters
雨票.

## Risky unavailable concepts to avoid
Detailed court language.

## Chapter hook
The witness remembers a door that was not there.

## End-of-chapter change
The case now has a human cost.
