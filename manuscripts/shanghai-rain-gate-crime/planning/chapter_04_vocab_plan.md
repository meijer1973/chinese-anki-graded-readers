# Chapter 04 Vocabulary Plan

## Chapter purpose
Open the fantasy layer through a recurring place.

## Scene goal
林安 follows the clue to the shadow door.

## Conflict
The place invites her in but threatens memory and safety.

## Emotional turn
Fear becomes action.

## Case function
Impossible clue.

## Journalist function
Observe and record details.

## Fantasy function
影门 appears under rain.

## Learning function
Repeat: 影门, 雨票, 观察, 痕迹, 白光.

## Main locations
Old building, doorway, riverside street.

## Characters and roles
林安: journalist. Unknown follower: pressure.

## Core known words to reuse
门, 外面, 里面, 光, 黑, 走, 进去.

## Stretch words to introduce
- 影门: recurring fantasy place.
- 痕迹: clue word bridging crime and fantasy.

## Stretch words to repeat from earlier chapters
雨票, 警察局, 确认.

## Risky unavailable concepts to avoid
Large magic-system explanations.

## Chapter hook
The door opens to a place that should not fit inside the building.

## End-of-chapter change
林安 knows the case is impossible but real.
