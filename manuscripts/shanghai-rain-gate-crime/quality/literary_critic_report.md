# Literary Critic Report

Score: 7.6 / 10

Decision: PASS with minor reservations.

The manuscript has a clear urban fantasy crime mechanism: a mundane Shanghai disappearance escalates into the discovery of a rain-operated shadow gate. The strongest features are the recurring locations, the use of professions that affect plot behavior, and the way the small alley changes meaning over the book. The prose remains simple because of the controlled vocabulary, but each chapter has a concrete change and the book avoids becoming a dialogue-only validation exercise.

Reservations: some sentences remain deliberately plain for graded-reader readability, and the crime resolution is simplified. These are acceptable for the target level.
