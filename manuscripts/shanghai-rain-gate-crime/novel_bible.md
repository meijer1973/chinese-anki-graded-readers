# Novel Bible — 上海雨票案

Mode: low_fantasy_urban_shanghai.

Premise: A Shanghai crime reporter receives a strange rain ticket while investigating a missing courier. The ticket opens a shadow gate used by a company manager to hide witnesses and evidence.

Target reader: restricted-vocabulary Chinese graded-reader fiction using the active 1000-word known list plus approved stretch packs.

Point of view: first-person, 林安.

Core cast:
- 林安: journalist/narrator.
- 陈雨: police officer, practical and skeptical but committed to the case.
- 老马: bookstore owner and compromised gatekeeper.
- 许白: photographer who hid evidence before being trapped by the gate.
- 宋文: company manager and criminal antagonist.
- 阿海: missing courier.

Fantasy rule: 雨票 opens 影门 only under rain, light, and shadow. 影门 remembers voices. Misuse costs memory, voice, or life.

Strange object: 雨票 / 黑票.

Secret place: the 小巷 behind the 南京路 bookstore, later revealed as an 影门 access point.

Recurring non-home locations: 南京路, 地铁站, 咖啡店, 书店, 小巷, 外滩, 黄浦江, 人民广场, 老房子, 屋顶.

Location that changes meaning: 小巷 begins as an ordinary urban shortcut and becomes the crime mechanism and moral center of the book.
