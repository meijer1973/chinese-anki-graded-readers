# Characters — 上海镜街案

- 林安: journalist protagonist. Her ethical line sharpens: selection is unavoidable, but must answer to sources, evidence, editors, law, and public responsibility.
- 陈雨: police contact. Works with ordinary evidence and procedural timing, while acknowledging private/fantasy-linked facts cannot enter reports directly.
- 何青: editor. Strongly active in headline choice, legal caution, source protection, and public-interest framing.
- 方舟: public suspect whose false public face is manufactured.
- 方小雨: 方舟's dead sister; her privacy becomes an ethical limit for the article.
- 谢宁: dead 清镜公司 employee who tried to stop the 镜街 project.
- 罗雨: missing witness and former 清镜 employee; protected as a source.
- 许澄: 清镜公司副总 and visible corporate antagonist.
- 白策 / 白先生: arc antagonist; argues that managing public truth is protection.
- 许白: source who provides older context and the record-mouth map.
- 老赵: mechanism-aware repairman; explains limits without solving the case.
- 阿洛: technical source who helps open and interpret files.
