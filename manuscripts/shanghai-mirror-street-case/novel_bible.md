# Novel Bible — 上海镜街案

- Series: 林安 journalist urban-fantasy crime
- Position: Book 9 after 上海旧城门案
- Primary mechanism: 镜街 / 第二面
- Public case: 清镜公司 and 明面传媒 manufacture public guilt through bought accounts, edited video, reputation contracts, and witness intimidation.
- Fantasy rule: 镜街 does not simply reflect bodies. The first mirror reflects the person; the second mirror reflects what repeated public voices make the city believe about that person.
- Cost: the victim can lose their public face, credibility, and ability to be believed. Witnesses may become unusable if their "second face" is released.
- Publishable evidence: contracts, accounts, payment routes, vehicle records, door-access logs, server material, witness testimony, recordings, and seized devices.
- Private/fantasy evidence: second mirror behavior, faces changing in reflections, 明日报 hook.
- Arc movement: 旧城门 concerns names/records; 镜街 concerns faces/public identity. 白策's managed-truth logic is challenged more directly.
- Next hook: 明日报 / tomorrow's newspaper.
