# Creative Preflight — 上海镜街案

## Premise alternatives considered

1. 镜街 as a simple haunted mirror street.
2. 镜街 as a company-controlled reputation market.
3. 镜街 as a public-rumor mechanism connected to 旧城门 and record mouths.

Chosen: alternative 3, because it gives the story a complete procedural case while moving the larger arc forward.

## Deliberate differences from the previous story

- Previous case centered on property/person records and 旧城门. This case centers on public face, online rumor, bought comments, edited identity, and mirror/reflection evidence.
- The emotional wound is public misrecognition: a person becomes what the crowd already wants to believe.
- The newsroom function is stronger: the article structure, headline, legal review, and protected-source decisions become part of the plot.
- 白策 pressures 林安 ideologically rather than mainly through direct property-record erasure.
- Ending type: the local case is solved, but the next mechanism appears as 明日报 / tomorrow's paper.

## Reader question

Can 林安 expose a company that sells public faces without turning the victims into another spectacle?

## Main pressure

方舟 is publicly accused because 镜街 and bought accounts make him appear guilty. 罗雨 is a witness whose own face and credibility are being threatened.

## Planned reversals

- The strongest video evidence is real but does not show the real person.
- 清镜公司 claims to repair public image but actually sells replacement faces.
- The publishable article must not include the most fantastic or most emotionally explosive material.
- 白策's critique of 林安 contains a partial truth, forcing her to articulate a better ethical rule.

## Variation budget

Procedural case first; mythology second. 镜街 is explained through evidence and harm, not as a lore lecture.
