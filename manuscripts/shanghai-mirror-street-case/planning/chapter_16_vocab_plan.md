# Chapter 16 Vocabulary Plan — 第十六章 可以 公开 的 路

- Case function: ordinary evidence action
- Journalist function: publish evidence without spectacle
- Fantasy function: fantasy excluded from article
- Learning function: repeat journalism/crime words such as 记者, 采访, 证据, 文件, 录音, 录像, 合同, 账号, 来源, 公开, 保护, 证人; repeat series terms 镜街, 第二面, 旧城门, 记录口 where needed.
- New-word control: use only a small set of book-specific terms repeatedly: 镜街, 第二面, 清镜, 脸价表, 封脸镜, 明日报.
