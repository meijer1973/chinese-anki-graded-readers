# Creative Preflight - 上海静水名单案

## Premise alternatives considered
1. A false insurance-company public scandal where the fantasy object only predicts contracts.
2. A murder case built around a river mirror.
3. A source-protection story where the fantasy mechanism identifies vulnerable witnesses.
4. A black-market list story where the crime is fraud rather than a corpse-first mystery.

## Chosen story shape
A longer financial-crime / insurance-fraud case in which `静水` and `水镜` expose a `名单` of vulnerable people. The fantasy clue points 林安 toward real evidence, but the publishable story must rely on files, contracts, witness statements, police evidence, money routes, and reporting ethics.

## Reader question
Can 林安 expose 静海公司 without turning protected victims and sources into public names?

## Variation budget from previous 林安 story
- Starts from reader comments and fraud paperwork, not a supernatural message sent directly to 林安.
- The emotional wound is naming/source protection.
- Antagonist pressure is corporate/legal/financial pressure.
- Fantasy mechanism is still water and water mirror, not sound/ringtone/recording.
- Location ecosystem includes apartment, market, bank, company building, warehouse, library basement, river, police station, park.
- Ending type includes public-service follow-up and victim aftercare, not only arrest.
