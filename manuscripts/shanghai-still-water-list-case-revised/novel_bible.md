# Novel Bible - 上海静水名单案

林安 remains a Shanghai journalist/crime reporter. 陈雨 remains the recurring police contact. 何青 remains editor/newsroom pressure.

Core fantasy mechanism: `静水` becomes unnaturally still. `水镜` reveals a `名单` of people whose fear can be exploited. Criminals use the list to target insurance-contract fraud victims.

Crime frame: 静海公司 uses fear, forged or pressured contracts, hidden client pages, bank/insurance paperwork, and witness pressure.

Moral dilemma: publishing names would dramatize the case but expose victims and sources. The final article protects names and focuses on method, evidence, and public warning.
