## Core Mystery

Who is using stolen voice recordings and the 回声盒子 to create false contracts, silence witnesses, and redirect public blame?

## Fantasy Rule

The 回声盒子 does not create lies. It turns hidden, feared, or future-possible speech into ringtone-like voices. It can mislead by timing and selection, but it cannot serve as legal proof by itself.
