# Literary Critic Report — 上海零点铃声案

## Score

8.3 / 10

## Strengths

- The fantasy mechanism is small and easy to follow: the 回声盒子 turns hidden/future-possible speech into ringtone clues but cannot act as legal proof.
- 林安 functions as a journalist: she interviews, verifies, protects witnesses, controls publication, and uses an article as an ethical tool.
- The personal-known vocabulary materially improves the adult crime texture: black market, hacker, bank, insurance, contract, customer, website, screen, apartment, and publication pressure.
- The case has a visible suspect and a deeper operator, preventing a flat clue chain.
- The ending turns publication into a moral decision rather than a simple case summary.

## Weaknesses / Risks

- The chapters are short and serial.
- Some syntax remains simple to preserve the controlled vocabulary.
- Sound/voice terms repeat often, but the repetition is structurally justified.

## Decision

PASS. This is stronger than a merely valid draft because the wider vocabulary changes what kinds of scenes are possible.
