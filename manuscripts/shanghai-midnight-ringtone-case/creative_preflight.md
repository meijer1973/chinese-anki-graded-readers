# Creative Preflight — 上海零点铃声案

## 3-5 Premise Or Scene Alternatives

1. **Voice-black-market case.** A dead hacker leaves files about stolen voices; an impossible box plays future or hidden calls. 林安 must protect witnesses while proving a crime that sounds like fantasy.
2. **Subway-door case.** A subway door opens into the wrong station whenever a witness lies. Rejected because it repeats the previous bridge/door movement pattern too closely.
3. **False-video case.** CCTV shows 林安 committing a crime before she arrives. Rejected because it would require too much technical vocabulary.
4. **Haunted newspaper case.** Headlines appear before crimes happen. Rejected because it makes 林安 receive clues too passively.
5. **Black-market contract case without fantasy.** Rejected because it would underuse the series promise.

## Chosen Story Shape

A Shanghai voice-black-market case. The impossible mechanism is the **回声盒子**: it does not create lies, but it turns hidden, feared, or future-possible speech into ringtone-like voices. 林安 cannot publish fantasy as evidence, so she must separate useful clues from legal proof and protect victims whose voices have been stolen.

## Why This Is Stronger Than The Alternatives

The personal-known layer supports richer adult crime scenes: 黑市, 黑客, 网络, 监控, 银行, 保险, 信用卡, 合同, 客户, 资料, 文件, 屏幕, 邮件, 公寓. The journalism/crime pack supports real reporting action rather than decoration.

## Core Reader Question

Can 林安 prove who is stealing people’s voices without publishing a witness into danger or trusting fantasy evidence too directly?

## Main Character Want

林安 wants to expose the voice-black-market crime while protecting 宋岚 and affected clients.

## Main Pressure

A fake article is about to publish under 林安’s name; the police need legal evidence; the witness may be harmed if named publicly; 老赵 and 顾严 both want the 回声盒子.

## Planned Reversals

1. A fake headline points toward 陈雨.
2. A visible evidence trail points toward 顾严.
3. The recovered files are true, but the direction is false.
4. 老赵 is not a harmless repairman.
5. 林安’s article becomes a protective tool rather than a simple exposé.

## Emotional Movement

林安 moves from fear of manipulation by her own voice to a more mature reporting stance: public enough to help, restrained enough not to harm.

## Vocabulary Feasibility Notes

This manuscript uses Marcel personalized mode. The personal-known layer is low-load known vocabulary for Marcel and is reported separately from stretch vocabulary.

## Public-Quality Risks

Short chapters, repeated sound vocabulary, and simple syntax. These are mitigated by concrete scene functions, witness pressure, and a recurring safety-rule motif.

## Variation Budget

- Investigation structure: source-protection and voice-black-market route, not bridge/property movement.
- Emotional wound: stolen voice and false public speech.
- Antagonist pressure: visible suspect plus deeper operator.
- Fantasy mechanism: ringtone/voice, not ticket/lamp/bridge.
- Location ecosystem: newsroom, subway, park, bank, hotel, old shop, river, underground room.
- Moral dilemma: publish names or publish methods while protecting witnesses.
- Ending type: public-service article plus witness protection.
