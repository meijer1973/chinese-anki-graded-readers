# Literary critic report — 上海灵灯案

Score: 7.6 / 10

Strengths:
- Clear continuation of 林安 as journalist rather than generic mystery narrator.
- The fantasy mechanism is small, repeated, and tied to memory, voice, shadow, and evidence.
- Publication ethics are present: the protagonist repeatedly chooses not to expose the witness.
- The locations have plot function: old street, police station, newsroom, subway, bookshop, underground, and rooftop.

Weaknesses:
- Dialogue tags remain necessarily simple under the active vocabulary.
- Some abstractions are simplified because the 1000-word core list plus stretch layers cannot support full legal/procedural nuance.

Decision: usable as a graded reader and not merely a validation exercise.
