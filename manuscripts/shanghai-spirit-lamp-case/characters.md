# Characters — 上海灵灯案

- 林安: journalist and narrator. Continuity-locked as the series protagonist.
- 陈雨: police officer and recurring contact. He insists on legal evidence and safety.
- 何青: editor. She forces publication discipline and protects the witness by limiting what can be public.
- 阿梅: witness/source. Her memory is unstable because of the lamp; her safety is central.
- 老吴: guard/victim. His death opens the case; his records point toward the files.
- 陆明: manager/suspect. Wants the old-street property and uses the lamp to alter witness pressure.
- 老马: bookstore owner/gatekeeper. Compromised but useful; opens the underground path.
- 许白: photographer/source. Connects the case to the earlier rain-ticket continuity.
