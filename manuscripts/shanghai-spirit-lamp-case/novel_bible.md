# Novel Bible — 上海灵灯案

Mode: low_fantasy_urban_shanghai.

Series: 林安 journalist urban-fantasy crime continuity.

Premise: After the rain-ticket case, journalist 林安 receives an anonymous file about a spirit lamp connected to an old Shanghai street death. The lamp can open memory and steal voice or shadow. A building manager uses it to pressure witnesses, alter video, and turn public reporting against the victim.

Target reader: restricted-vocabulary Chinese graded-reader fiction using the active 1000-word known list plus approved stretch packs.

Point of view: first person, 林安.

Core cast:
- 林安: journalist/narrator; wants truth but must protect sources.
- 陈雨: police officer; pushes legal evidence and safety.
- 何青: editor; pressures 林安 to publish responsibly.
- 阿梅: witness and protected source.
- 老吴: dead guard and victim.
- 陆明: office-building manager and suspect.
- 老马: bookstore owner and compromised gatekeeper.
- 许白: photographer/source from earlier continuity.

Fantasy rule: 灵灯 works through rain, shadow, and memory. It can reveal a hidden memory, but may take voice, shadow, or an important recollection. It can create false images when it has touched a person's object. It cannot itself be used as legal evidence; it can only point toward evidence.

Strange object: 灵灯.

Secret place: the underground street below the 南京路 bookshop and old-street shop.

Recurring non-home/non-school/non-hospital locations: 老街, 黄浦江, 警察局, 咖啡店, 地铁站, 办公楼, 南京路书店, 小巷, 地下, 屋顶.

Location that changes meaning: 老街 begins as an ordinary shop street and becomes the visible surface of the underground memory archive.
