# Literary Critic Report — 上海旧城门案

Score: 8.2 / 10

The story preserves the procedural engine while advancing the longer arc. The central case is concrete: a worker/source dies after exposing manipulated property/person records, a missing guard leaves a log trail, and a company uses contracts, money, intimidation, and falsified records to remove residents from their own public existence.

The fantasy mechanism is thematically aligned. `旧城门` does not solve the case; it clarifies the series mythology by connecting earlier record-mouth mechanisms while keeping the publishable evidence ordinary.

The strongest element is the ethical conflict: 林安 recognizes that ethical journalism also edits and protects, but rejects 白策's conversion of protection into private rule. Relationship development with 陈雨 and 何青 is controlled and functional rather than melodramatic.

Weakness: the story is still constrained by controlled-vocabulary directness, so some transitions remain explicit. This is acceptable for the target reader and better than opaque prose.

Decision: PASS
