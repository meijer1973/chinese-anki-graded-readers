# Novel Bible — 上海旧城门案

## One-line premise

After the silent-archive case points toward `旧城门`, 林安 investigates a death on 青石老街 and uncovers a company using altered property records, intimidation, and a hidden city gate to make people lose their names, homes, and public existence.

## Case

- Dead worker/source: 秦海.
- Missing witness/guard: 梁叔.
- Visible company: 青门公司.
- Visible executive suspect: 顾澄.
- False-confession pressure: 马林 is forced to confess to protect his child.
- Publishable evidence: phone-card recovery, email, video, bank/payment records, contract records, warehouse labels, witness testimony, fibers/blood trace, security logs.

## Fantasy mechanism

`旧城门` is a larger passage behind earlier `记录口` mechanisms. It can make records cross between public files and hidden civic memory. It is not legal proof; it guides 林安 toward ordinary evidence.

## Arc function

白策/白先生 appears directly and argues that truth must be managed to protect people. 林安 rejects his control while recognizing that ethical journalism must protect vulnerable witnesses. The next clue points toward `镜街`.

## Theme

Protection is not the same as control. A journalist may hide names to limit harm, but must not hide criminal methods, power, or responsibility.

## Continuity

林安 remains the journalist protagonist. 陈雨 remains the recurring police contact. 何青 becomes a more active editorial/evidence partner. 许白, 阿洛, 老赵, and 唐岚 remain continuity-linked sources.
