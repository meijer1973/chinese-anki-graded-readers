# Creative Preflight — 上海旧城门案

## Series position

Book 8 in the 林安 journalist urban-fantasy crime sequence, following `上海无声档案案`.

## Deliberate variation budget

1. **Investigation structure:** this case moves from an archive/data-erasure case to a physical old-street/property-record case. The publishable evidence chain is built from housing records, contracts, bank/payment traces, phone-card recovery, witness interviews, and police searches.
2. **Fantasy mechanism:** the primary mechanism is `旧城门`, not another small object. It is still handled as a low-fantasy civic-record mechanism: the gate moves records and names; it does not solve the crime.
3. **Antagonist pressure:** 白策 appears directly and frames truth-management as protection, forcing 林安 to distinguish ethical source protection from authoritarian control.
4. **Relationship development:** 林安 and 陈雨 move toward higher procedural trust; 何青 moves from cautious editor to active evidence-protection partner.
5. **Arc movement:** the hidden record system becomes clearer through `记录口`, but the book remains a concrete murder/property-intimidation case.

## Core promise

A normal Shanghai case opens one impossible door. 林安 investigates as a journalist, uses fantasy clues only as direction, builds publishable/legal evidence through ordinary methods, protects sources, and resists 白策's managed truth.

## Risk controls

- No lore dump.
- No epic-fantasy kingdom/race/monster expansion.
- Keep each chapter tied to case evidence, witness pressure, police procedure, or publication ethics.
- Avoid microchapters; 17 chapters average more than 400 word tokens.
