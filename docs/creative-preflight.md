# Creative Preflight

Create `manuscripts/<slug>/creative_preflight.md` before vocabulary planning. This file protects the story from becoming merely the first vocabulary-feasible idea.

The creative preflight happens before chapter-level token planning. It should explore story possibility first, then choose a shape that can be mapped to approved vocabulary.

## Required Sections

```markdown
# Creative Preflight

## 3-5 Premise Or Scene Alternatives

## Rejected Ideas And Why

## Chosen Story Shape

## Why This Is Stronger Than The Alternatives

## Core Reader Question

## Main Character Want

## Main Pressure

## Planned Reversals

## Emotional Movement

## Vocabulary Feasibility Notes

## Public-Quality Risks

## Variation Budget
```

## Variation Budget

For a sequel, name at least three ways this book differs from the previous one:

- investigation structure
- emotional wound
- antagonist pressure
- fantasy mechanism
- location ecosystem
- moral dilemma
- narrative rhythm
- ending type

For the 林安 series, the variation budget must not reboot continuity. 林安 remains the journalist protagonist and 陈雨 remains the recurring police contact unless the user explicitly approves a continuity change.

## Review Rule

A plausible premise is not enough. The chosen shape should have:

- a concrete situation;
- a character want;
- pressure or conflict;
- a discovery or reversal path;
- an ending pressure;
- a reason for the reader to continue.
