# 断剑山门 Recurring Objects

## 断剑

Central object. It should appear in most volumes, but its function should change: status, guide, test, danger, witness, and finally silence.

## Old Records

Use records, missing pages, cut-out names, and contradictory dates to keep the mystery concrete.

## 灵石

Use damaged 灵石 to connect spiritual power with politics and economy.

## 符箓 / 黑符

Use talisman vocabulary for court pressure, hidden control, and false authority.

## 丹药

Use pills for healing, temptation, cost, and unfair access to cultivation resources.

## Sect Tokens And Oaths

Use public signs of authority only when they change what a character can do: entry, punishment, challenge, exile, oath, confession.
