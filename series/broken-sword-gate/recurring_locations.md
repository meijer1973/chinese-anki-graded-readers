# 断剑山门 Recurring Locations

## 青岚山门

Public honor, hierarchy, punishment, arrival scenes, and visible sect identity.

## 剑阁

Old swords, the broken sword, early mystery, and private tests of worth.

## 藏经阁

Training texts, old records, official history, and missing evidence.

## 丹房

Healing, damaged disciples, cultivation cost, and practical consequences.

## 试剑台

Public training, shame, rivalry, 比武, and visible change in skill.

## 后山禁地

Old seals, strange wind, first supernatural danger, and the path toward 归墟谷.

## 白鹿镇

Ordinary people, merchants, wounded villagers, and evidence that sect choices affect non-cultivators.

## 王都

Court pressure, official documents, soldiers, reputation, and temptation.

## 归墟谷

Damaged spiritual place under 白鹿山; remembered as the prison of 魔尊 but actually tied to the wounded 灵脉.
