# 断剑山门 Series Bible

## Series Identity

- Series slug: `broken-sword-gate`
- Chinese series title: `断剑山门`
- English working title: `The Broken Sword Gate`
- Genre: ancient sword-sect cultivation fantasy with wuxia discipline.
- Default vocabulary mode: public controlled mode unless a manuscript explicitly chooses Marcel personalized mode.
- Default packs: `data/stretch_packs/general_fiction_150.txt` and `data/stretch_packs/fantasy_232.txt`.
- Core promise: a weak new disciple enters a declining sword sect, receives a broken sword, and learns that loyalty to a sect may require exposing the sect's founding lie.

This continuity is not a low-fantasy continuity. It may use sect politics, cultivation stages, rival factions, court pressure, battles, monsters, invented institutions, and complex magic when the words are in core, stretch, book-specific, proper-noun, or configured learner-profile layers.

## Central Question

Can 沈青禾 save 青岚剑宗 without becoming another person who hides truth behind honor?

## World Frame

The story takes place in the old kingdom 大梁. The royal court cannot fully control the sword sects because the sects guard mountain 灵脉. The court needs sect power in danger, but fears it in peace.

The old agreement is weakening:

- sects may train disciples;
- sects may guard their own mountains;
- sects may not raise private armies;
- the court may not enter a sect's 禁地;
- major disputes are settled by public 比武 and judgment from the 仙门盟.

## Main Region

The first half of the series centers on 白鹿山 and the declining 青岚剑宗. Important recurring places include 青岚山门, 剑阁, 藏经阁, 丹房, 试剑台, 后山禁地, 白鹿镇, 王都, and 归墟谷.

## Cultivation Scope

Use only a small number of stages unless a later vocabulary plan explicitly expands them:

- 练气: beginner stage; feeling and guiding 灵气.
- 筑基: stable foundation; stronger body and sword control.
- 金丹: rare high stage; politically important.
- 元婴: legendary level; mentioned more than used.

Sword power has three readable layers: 剑招, 剑气, and 剑意.

## Series Arc

1. 青禾 wants strength and recognition.
2. The broken sword gives her status and access to hidden truth.
3. Sect history, court pressure, and rival factions show that every group wants control of 灵脉.
4. 青禾 learns that a sword can cut enemies, seals, lies, memories, debts, and false versions of the self.
5. The end state should not be "青禾 becomes the strongest tyrant." The end state is a changed 山门 and a new oath about public truth.

## Ten-Volume Spine

1. `断剑入山`: 青禾 enters 青岚剑宗 and the broken sword speaks her family name.
2. `试剑台`: training and public shame; 青禾 cuts a hidden 禁制 by accident.
3. `藏经阁旧事`: old records expose cracks in the founding story.
4. `白石比武`: rival sect pressure; 韩照 becomes a meaningful rival.
5. `妖市灵石`: damaged 灵石 reveal resource theft and wider pressure.
6. `王都黑符`: court temptation and political coercion.
7. `山门内乱`: internal betrayal and the first major confession.
8. `归墟谷`: the sealed valley reveals the mythic reversal.
9. `天雷剑阵`: court and sects try to seize the 灵脉.
10. `新山门`: 青禾 breaks the false seal and helps create a new rule for the sects.
