# 断剑山门 Character Registry

## 沈青禾

- Role: protagonist; new disciple of 青岚剑宗.
- Background: poor village girl from near 白鹿山; her parents died after black wind and sword light.
- Outer want: become a real sword disciple.
- Inner want: prove she was not saved by mistake.
- Hidden need: learn that gratitude does not require silence.
- Arc: from weak disciple seeking approval to sword cultivator who rebuilds the meaning of 山门.

## 江白

- Role: senior disciple and 师兄 figure.
- Function: friend, rival, and moral foil.
- Outer want: restore 青岚剑宗's honor.
- Inner fear: without the sect, he is nobody.
- Arc: from rule-bound defender of reputation to someone willing to defend truth even when it shames the sect.

## 韩照

- Role: rival from 白石剑派.
- Function: early antagonist, later unwilling ally.
- Outer want: defeat 青禾 publicly and prove 白石 superiority.
- Inner wound: trained to believe mercy creates failure.

## 云澜

- Role: 掌门 of 青岚剑宗.
- Function: mentor, obstacle, tragic authority figure.
- Outer want: keep the sect alive.
- Inner fear: truth will make every sacrifice meaningless.

## 莫长老

- Role: conservative elder inside 青岚剑宗.
- Function: internal antagonist and betrayal pressure.
- Belief: weak disciples should serve quietly and sect authority matters more than individual truth.

## 陆衡

- Role: court envoy and main human antagonist.
- Function: political antagonist with a defensible argument.
- Belief: spiritual power should be under court control because ordinary people suffer when sects fight.

## 祖师残魂

- Role: remnant voice inside the broken sword.
- Function: guide, training source, and source of misdirection.
- Secret: he sealed more than a 魔尊; he sealed his own guilt.

## 夜无归

- Role: sealed "魔尊."
- Function: feared final enemy at first, then wounded witness.
- Hidden possibility: once guarded the mountain 灵脉 or resisted the first theft of spiritual power.
