# 断剑山门 Mechanism Registry

## 灵气 And 灵脉

- Rule: cultivation depends on 灵气. Strong mountains, old trees, rivers, caves, and underground lines may hold it.
- Series pressure: sects are political powers because they guard 灵脉.
- Cost: weakening 灵脉 harms sect power and ordinary people below the mountain.

## 灵根

- Rule: disciples are judged by the strength of their 灵根.
- 青禾's apparent weakness: her 灵根 looks weak by ordinary tests.
- Hidden gift: she can hear movement in 剑气 and feel false seals.

## Sword Cultivation

- 剑招: visible sword moves.
- 剑气: energy carried through the sword.
- 剑意: deeper intent behind the sword.
- 青禾's risk: she reaches toward 剑意 before her body is ready.

## 断剑

- Object: old broken sword with half its blade missing.
- Powers: hears damaged 灵脉, opens old 禁制, cuts false 剑气, shows fragments of founding history, grows stronger when 青禾 tells truth.
- Cost: forced use damages 青禾's 灵根; angry use lets black 剑气 enter her body; using it to protect a lie makes it silent.
- Thematic rule: the sword should not only kill. It can cut seals, lies, debts, memories, and false versions of the self.

## 封印 And 禁制

- Rule: seals can protect, imprison, hide truth, or steal power.
- Series mystery: the founder's seal may have protected people and also preserved an institutional crime.

## 心魔, 魔气, 黑气

- Inner danger: 心魔 is pride, shame, revenge, fear, and hunger to be seen as strong.
- Outer pressure: 魔气 or 黑气 leaks from the old seal in 归墟谷.
- 青禾's specific temptation: "If they looked down on you, you have the right to hurt them."
