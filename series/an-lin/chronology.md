# 林安 Series Chronology

## Before Series

林安 works as a journalist in Shanghai. She knows that truth can help people, but it can also bring danger when powerful people want silence.

## Book 1: 上海雨票案

- Repository path: `manuscripts/shanghai-rain-gate-crime/`
- Status: first series manuscript imported from the writer agent.
- Core continuity: 林安 is the journalist protagonist. 陈雨 is a recurring police contact.
- Fantasy mechanism: rain-ticket and shadow-gate imagery tied to crime clues and memory.
- Outcome: the story establishes that Shanghai crime cases can touch impossible mechanisms.

## Book 2: 上海灵灯案

- Repository path: `manuscripts/shanghai-spirit-lamp-case/`
- Status: second series manuscript imported from the writer agent and revalidated locally.
- Core continuity: 林安 remains the journalist protagonist. 陈雨 remains the recurring police contact.
- Recurring support: 何青 appears as editor/newsroom pressure; 许白 remains a source/contact from earlier continuity.
- Fantasy mechanism: 灵灯 works through rain, shadow, memory, voice, and altered images.
- Case frame: old Shanghai street death, witness pressure, altered video, source protection, and publication ethics.
- Outcome: the series now has a stronger journalist-crime procedure pattern: interview, verify, protect source, publish carefully, and separate fantasy clues from legal evidence.

## Open Series Direction

The next story should not restart 林安 from zero. It should treat her as someone who has already faced the rain-ticket case and the spirit-lamp case, and now knows that a normal clue may have a hidden cost.
