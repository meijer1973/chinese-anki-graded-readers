# 林安 Series Bible

## Series Identity

- Series protagonist: 林安
- Stable role: 记者 / crime reporter
- Recurring contact: 陈雨, 警察 / police contact
- Genre: urban fantasy crime
- Default setting: Shanghai, especially ordinary work spaces, streets, old buildings, river areas, and police/newsroom locations.
- Core promise: each story begins with a Shanghai crime case and one impossible low-fantasy mechanism.
- Do not reset continuity unless the user explicitly requests a reboot.

## Core Story Pattern

A normal case in Shanghai opens a small impossible door. 林安 investigates as a journalist: she interviews people, checks sources, protects vulnerable witnesses, and decides what can be made public. The fantasy layer should make the case stranger and more emotionally dangerous, not turn the story into epic fantasy.

## Established Protagonist

林安 is now the journalist protagonist for the series. Do not rewrite her as a delivery worker, student, doctor, or one-off viewpoint character unless a new continuity note explicitly changes the series.

She is brave but not reckless. She wants truth, but she must also decide when publishing a truth can hurt someone. Her strongest scenes should show her making choices, not only receiving clues.

## Current Arc Pressure

By `上海镜街案`, 林安 has seen that several impossible places act like record mouths: rain tickets, spirit lamps, shadow bridges, still water, the lost-property locker, silent archives, the old city gate, and now mirror street. The larger pressure is no longer only one company or one case. 白先生 / 白策 and 旧档会 try to manage what the city remembers, what becomes public, which witnesses are allowed to keep their own records, and what public face a person is allowed to have.

Future stories should keep the case concrete, but they may let this hidden record system become clearer a little at a time. `旧城门` and `镜街` are now established mechanisms, and `明日报` is the next visible clue. Handle it through grounded newsroom/case pressure rather than sudden mythology dumps.

## Continuity Rule

Future manuscripts in this series must read this file, `character_registry.md`, `chronology.md`, `mechanism_registry.md`, `open_threads.md`, `recurring_locations.md`, `recurring_objects.md`, `sequel_constraints.md`, and `series_update_log.md` before planning. If a writer wants to contradict an established fact, they must create a visible continuity note and ask for approval.

After an accepted 林安 story passes vocabulary validation, lead quality review, and EPUB build if applicable, update the series memory package before planning the next story:

- add a concise entry to `chronology.md`;
- add only durable character facts to `character_registry.md`;
- update `mechanism_registry.md` with the fantasy rule, cost, limit, and relationship to earlier mechanisms;
- close or add questions in `open_threads.md`;
- update this bible only if the stable identity or current arc pressure changed;
- append an audit entry to `series_update_log.md`.
