# 林安 Series Bible

## Series Identity

- Series protagonist: 林安
- Stable role: 记者 / crime reporter
- Recurring contact: 陈雨, 警察 / police contact
- Genre: urban fantasy crime
- Default setting: Shanghai, especially ordinary work spaces, streets, old buildings, river areas, and police/newsroom locations.
- Core promise: each story begins with a Shanghai crime case and one impossible low-fantasy mechanism.
- Do not reset continuity unless the user explicitly requests a reboot.

## Core Story Pattern

A normal case in Shanghai opens a small impossible door. 林安 investigates as a journalist: she interviews people, checks sources, protects vulnerable witnesses, and decides what can be made public. The fantasy layer should make the case stranger and more emotionally dangerous, not turn the story into epic fantasy.

## Established Protagonist

林安 is now the journalist protagonist for the series. Do not rewrite her as a delivery worker, student, doctor, or one-off viewpoint character unless a new continuity note explicitly changes the series.

She is brave but not reckless. She wants truth, but she must also decide when publishing a truth can hurt someone. Her strongest scenes should show her making choices, not only receiving clues.

## Recurring Fantasy Terms

These terms are approved for recurring series use when they are also included in the active manuscript vocabulary files:

- 雨票
- 黑票
- 影门
- 灵灯
- 影子桥
- 黑雾
- 白光

Use a small number of fantasy terms repeatedly. Do not add a new magic system for each chapter.

## Continuity Rule

Future manuscripts in this series must read this file, `character_registry.md`, `chronology.md`, and `sequel_constraints.md` before planning. If a writer wants to contradict an established fact, they must create a visible continuity note and ask for approval.
