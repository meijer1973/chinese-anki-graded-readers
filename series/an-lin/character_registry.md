# 林安 Character Registry

## 林安

- Stable role: journalist / crime reporter
- Series function: investigates Shanghai crime cases that touch a small fantasy layer.
- Core want: find and publish the truth without harming people who trusted her.
- Strengths: careful observation, persistence, empathy for witnesses and victims.
- Risks: can push too hard, can place herself in danger, can confuse public truth with personal duty.
- Continuity lock: she is not a delivery worker in this series continuity.

## 陈雨

- Stable role: police officer and recurring police contact.
- Series function: gives procedural pressure, legal limits, and access to the police side of a case.
- Relationship with 林安: tense but useful trust. They can disagree about publication, secrecy, and danger.
- Continuity lock: do not make 陈雨 a random one-scene helper.

## Supporting Roles To Prefer

Use varied roles that affect the plot:

- editor
- witness
- informant
- shop owner
- landlord
- neighbor
- guard
- photographer
- reader
- suspect

Avoid making every important person a teacher, doctor, student, or generic police officer.
