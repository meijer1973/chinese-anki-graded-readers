# 林安 Recurring Locations

Use recurring locations as story tools, not decoration.

## Established / Preferred Location Types

- newsroom or newspaper office
- police station
- Huangpu River area
- old Shanghai building
- small shop or cafe
- subway station
- narrow lane
- riverside bridge
- archive or file room

## Location Continuity Guidance

Each new story should use at least one location that changes meaning by the end. For example, a cafe can begin as a safe interview place and become the place where a source disappears.

Do not set every scene at home, school, or hospital.
